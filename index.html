The method

Three passes over every claim

01

Read the claim

A model weighs the wording, flags loaded or unfalsifiable language, and lays out what would need to be true.

02

Search the web

It runs live Google searches, reads what it finds, and cross-checks the claim against primary sources and prior fact-checks.

03

Attach the sources

You get a verdict with a confidence figure and a trail of links you can open and read yourself.

What the verdicts mean

Plain labels, no spin

True

The claim matches the primary evidence located.

Misleading

Partly true but framed to mislead — wrong context, old image, cropped chart.

False

Contradicted by the evidence, or no credible source exists.

A fourth label — UNVERIFIED — is used when no primary source can be located. VerifyAI never guesses a verdict it can't support.
